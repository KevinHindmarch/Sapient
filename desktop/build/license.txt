SAPIENT - IMPORTANT NOTICE

Sapient is portfolio research software that runs entirely on this computer.
Your portfolios, settings and history are stored only in your Windows user
profile (%APPDATA%\Sapient). They are kept if you uninstall Sapient.

Market data comes from Yahoo Finance and is for research only. It may be
delayed or inaccurate and is not a price you can trade at.

Trading involves risk, including the loss of money. Sapient does not give
financial advice. You are responsible for every order placed in your
Interactive Brokers account.

Sapient never asks for or stores your Interactive Brokers password. You log in
to Trader Workstation (TWS) yourself. Sapient starts in simulation and
read-only modes; paper trading and real-money trading each need your explicit
permission and can be stopped at any time with the kill switch.

By clicking "I Agree" you confirm you have read and understood this notice.
